{{ ico("book", 30) }}Questions safety and how it works

Is pdtools safe to use?

Yes. There is no login and no wallet connect. The site never asks for your X password, cannot see your X account, and cannot post, like, follow or reply for you. It only shows public posts and links you to them on X. Anything you do after that, you do yourself on X, exactly like scrolling your normal feed.

Can using it get my X account flagged as spam?

The site itself never touches your account, so opening posts here is no different from opening them anywhere else. What X watches is behaviour: lots of identical replies, copy pasted text, or tagging the same accounts again and again in a short time. Keep it human and you are fine:

  • Write your own replies and change them up. The Say hi button only fills in a starting line, so edit it before you send.
  • Pace yourself. A handful of real replies beats fifty quick ones.
  • Only tag people when it adds something. You do not need to tag the team on every reply.
  • Like, reply or quote, whatever fits. Nobody here tracks or rewards how many you do.
What does the site know about me?

Nothing personal. The posts you have opened are remembered only in your own browser. The open counts you see are anonymous totals, with no names attached. Suggestions sent through the mailbox are private to the site owner and the form does not collect emails.

Why do I only see blue tick accounts?

It keeps bots and spam out of the feed, and it keeps the running cost down so the site can stay free.

Is this an official Pocket Dragons site?

No. pdtools is an unofficial fan made tool built by Jon Wynn for the Keeper community. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pocket Dragons, and every post links back to X.

How do I get into the Keepers directory?

Have a blue tick and the 🐉 in your X display name, then post about Pocket Dragons or use the Add me form on the Keepers page.

I have an idea or found a bug

Tap the mailbox in the corner. Every suggestion goes straight to Jon and is read.
