Privacy

What pdtools keeps, and why

pdtools is a free, unofficial community tool for Pocket Dragons Keepers, built by Jon Wynn. It is not run by the Pocket Dragons team. This page lists everything it stores about you.

Last updated 9 November 2026.

In short

  • No wallet, no password and no email address is ever asked for.
  • Most of your progress stays in your own browser, on your own device.
  • When you sign in with X, pdtools reads who you are and hands the sign in back to X straight away. It never posts for you.
  • Nothing is sold, and there are no ads or ad trackers.

What is stored

A random number your browser makes for itself, your device type (phone or computer), where your visit came from (for example X or Discord) and page counts. No name, X account or IP address.

Cookies and your browser

pdtools uses a few small cookies to keep you signed in, to protect forms from misuse, to remember your settings and to let preview testers see new pages early. Your opened posts, streak, dragons and language are saved in your own browser storage. Clearing your browser removes them.

Your IP address is used for a short time to stop floods of requests. It is not saved with your activity. The companies that host pdtools may keep it in their own logs.

Services that help run pdtools

  • Render hosts the website.
  • Cloudflare sits in front of the site to keep it fast and safe.
  • Supabase holds the database and runs the timers that check for new posts.
  • X handles sign in and supplies public posts.
  • Discord supplies posts shared in the Pocket Dragons content channel.
  • Cloudinary serves some images and sounds.

Your choices

You can ask to see what pdtools holds about you, or to have any of it removed, at any time. Send a direct message to @{{ cfg.author_x }} on X from the account it is about. Requests are handled as soon as possible.
