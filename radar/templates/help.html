{{ ico("hatch", 26) }}Keep your progress in one place

Your opened posts, streak and dragons are saved in the browser you use, on that device. There is no account, so a different browser starts fresh.

The X app opens links in its own browser, and that one keeps its own progress. To bring everything together:

  1. Inside the X app, open pdtools and tap Make my move code at the top of the feed. Copy the code.
  2. Open pdtools.xyz in your phone browser, tap Progress, enter the code and tap Restore.
  3. From then on, open pdtools in your phone browser. Inside X, use the menu and choose Open in browser.

Nothing is lost when this happens. Every post you open still counts as support for the person who posted it.
