How Keeper Art works

Monday to Sunday

Signed Keepers send in one hand made artwork each, with a link to a video of it being made.

Checked by hand

Jon watches the video and looks at the art. Only real, hand made Pocket Dragons art goes on the wall.

Friday to Sunday

Every signed Keeper gets one vote for their favourite. You can not vote for your own art.

Monday

The art with the most votes becomes Keeper Art of the week and joins the wall of winners.
